Questions

What exactly does the board show?

The day's strongest F&O stocks on each side, updated every minute, each with its score, futures open-interest reading and, for the leaders, the order block, support, resistance and liquidity level on a 5-minute chart.

Is this a tips service? Will it tell me what to buy?

No. {{SITE}} is a market analytics tool. It ranks and marks; it does not recommend buying or selling anything. What you trade, how much, and where you exit is your decision.

When is the board live?

On trading days from 09:15 to 15:30 IST. The ranking settles after the first few minutes, once there is enough price action. On weekends and holidays you see the last session's closing board.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It runs in the browser on phones, tablets and computers. Nothing to install.
